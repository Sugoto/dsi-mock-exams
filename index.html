    How to use

    • Each answer is graded as soon as you give it, with the correct answer and an explanation.
    • For numeric questions, type an exact whole number and press Enter or Check. Commas are fine (25,075 or 25075).
    • Finish and see score shows your total out of 50 and which topics you missed. Use Show only missed to review. Your answers are saved in this browser.

    Coverage

    • Relational model and SQL: keys, joins, projection, grouping, NULLs.
    • Transactions and recovery: ACID, logging, commit and abort.
    • Storage: serialization, C++ ownership and lifetimes, the storage hierarchy, page files.
    • Slotted pages, free lists and index construction.
    • Hash indexing: linear, quadratic and double probing, tombstones.
    • Buffer management: pinning, dirty pages, LRU, 2Q.
    • Multi-threading: races, mutexes, reader/writer locks, fine-grained locking.
    • Papers: Codd 1970; Stonebraker & Pavlo 2024; Stonebraker 1981; Neumann & Freitag 2020 (Umbra).
